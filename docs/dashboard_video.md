# RetailSignal dashboard tour

[▶ Watch or download the dashboard recording (MP4)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vidyacheekuri/retailsignal/main/docs/assets/retailsignal-dashboard-tour.mp4)

A 2 minute 35 second silent tour of the running Streamlit dashboard, captured on October 17, 2026. The recording shows the dashboard in a compact browser layout using the public UCI Online Retail case-study data. No account or local installation is needed to watch.

| Time | Workspace | Included in the recording |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Revenue overview | Financial metrics, monthly trends, markets, revenue bridge, identity coverage |
| 0:30 | Customer retention | Cohort heatmap, behavior segments, explainable rules |
| 0:48 | Decision lab | Scenario controls, adjusted conversion assumption, propensity distribution, customer queue |
| 1:19 | Model evidence | Holdout metrics, temporal evaluation, calibration, feature importance, limitations, drift |
| 1:55 | Data trust | Record reconciliation, cleaning policies, source lineage |
| 2:10 | Analyst desk | Business findings, question selection, supporting evidence, executive-brief download control |

The video is recorded from the live dashboard. Pauses between recording segments are shortened; no narration or music is included. MP4 chapter markers are available in players that support them. If your browser downloads the file, open it with your usual video player.

The campaign inputs are illustrative assumptions, and model scores estimate natural repeat purchase rather than causal campaign impact. Recording the dashboard does not change those limitations.
